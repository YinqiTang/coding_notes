# Mac Notes

Handy macOS shortcuts and tricks, especially for people coming from Windows.

## Right-Click & Editing

- **Right-click equivalent**: `Control` + click (or two-finger click — configurable in *System Settings → Trackpad*)
- **Paste without formatting**: `Option (⌥)` + `Shift` + `Command (⌘)` + `V`

## Search

- **Option 1**: the search icon in the top-right corner
- **Option 2**: `Command` + `Space`, then `Command` + `Return` to go to the location

## Moving Files (A → B)

- **Option 1**: drag and drop
- **Option 2**: `Command` + `C` to copy, `Option` + `Command` + `V` to move

## App Management

- **Show all running apps**: `Command` + `Tab`
- **Quit the app**: `Command` + `Q`
- **Close the app but keep it running in the background**: click the red ✕
- **Force quit software**: `Command (⌘)` + `Option` + `Escape`

## Screenshots

- **Screenshot (selection)**: `Command` + `Shift` + `4`
- **Full screenshot**: `Command` + `Shift` + `3`

## Time Machine

Shows the version history of files on your computer.

## Zoom

- **Zoom in**: `Cmd` + `+`
- **Zoom out**: `Cmd` + `-`

## Positron

- **Batch-comment several lines of code**: `Cmd` + `/`
